## Selected projects

### 01 / [CheckPOS — SaaS point of sale](projects/checkpos.md)
**Collaborative project · MVP in development**

A POS platform designed for Peruvian neighborhood stores and minimarkets, with a mobile-first approach and offline-first architecture.

- **Stack in the shared repository:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Go, PostgreSQL, Dexie / IndexedDB, Docker.
- **Current implementation:** multi-tenant data foundation with RLS, authentication, device binding, PIN access, and local storage models.
- **Next phases:** sales, customer credit (*fiados*), cash closing, and end-to-end synchronization.

[Technical case study](projects/checkpos.md) · [Team repository](https://github.com/AriasYarangaIam/CheckPOS)
